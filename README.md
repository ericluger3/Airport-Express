Flying into Buffalo to Visit Niagara Falls: Why It Can Save Money
Many visitors discover that flying into Buffalo Niagara International Airport can cost less than flying into Toronto, even when the destination is Canada. The airport is about 25 to 30 miles from Niagara Falls, so the extra ground leg is short.
The savings only hold if the ground transportation is planned well. A pre-booked ride with a clear price avoids surprise fees and long waits at the curb. Services such as Buffalo Airport Express offer private rides with applicable taxes, tolls and fees included in the quote.
To make the most of the trip:
Compare total flight plus ground costs, not just airfare
Book your ride before you land
Build in time for the border
Keep passports and documents ready
With a little planning, landing in Buffalo can be an easy and affordable way to reach the Falls.
